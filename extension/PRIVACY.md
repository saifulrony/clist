# Privacy Policy for Client List - Business Scraper

**Last updated:** September 2, 2026

## Overview
**Client List - Business Scraper** is a productivity and B2B lead generation browser extension designed to help businesses, researchers, and sales professionals collect publicly available business directory listings from platforms such as Google Maps, Yelp, and Yellow Pages directly into their self-hosted or managed ClientList database.

## Data Collection and Usage
1. **Public Business Data Only**: The extension only reads public business information (such as business name, public address, public telephone number, public email address, rating, and website URL) from the active webpage when the user explicitly clicks the "Extract" button.
2. **User-Initiated Action**: No scraping or browsing data collection happens automatically in the background. Operations are strictly initiated by the user.
3. **No Personal Browsing History**: The extension does NOT track, store, or transmit personal web browsing history, search history, cookies, passwords, or personal identity information.
4. **Data Destination**: Extracted leads are transmitted solely to the user's configured backend API endpoint (e.g., `http://localhost:18001` or the user's private server). We do NOT operate intermediate proxy servers or collect copies of your scraped data.

## Third-Party Sharing
- We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your scraped data or personal information to third parties or data brokers.
- We do **NOT** use your data for advertising, behavioral retargeting, or credit evaluation.

## Storage and Permissions
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to remember your preferred backend API URL and local session settings.
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used strictly to execute content scripts on supported directory pages upon your explicit command.
