Privacy Policy

Extension: Client List - Business Scraper
Version: 1.0.0
Effective Date: September 2, 2026

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Client List - Business Scraper is a B2B productivity tool. It operates exclusively when commanded by the user to extract publicly listed contact and location details (Name, Address, Phone, Website, Category, Rating) from directory services such as Google Maps and Yelp.

2. Data Handling & Storage

  • Local Direct Transfer: Data collected is transmitted directly to your own self-hosted or dedicated backend server (e.g. http://localhost:18001).
  • No Intermediaries: We do not route your data through third-party servers, analytics brokers, or remote logging services.
  • No Personal Tracking: The extension does not record your browsing habits, searches outside the active directory tab, or personal credentials.

3. Permissions Justification

  • activeTab & scripting: Necessary to query the directory page's DOM elements to capture business listing cards when you click "Extract".
  • storage: Used solely to retain your target backend API endpoint across sessions.

4. No Sale of User Data

We do not sell, license, or monetize any scraped business information or user data to advertisers, data brokers, or commercial syndicates.
